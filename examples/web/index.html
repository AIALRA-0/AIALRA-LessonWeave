A painted sign on loan

A museum lends a painted wooden sign for eight weeks. Keep the transport crate closed for the first 24 hours so the object can adjust gradually.

Light and inspection

Keep display lighting at 50 lux for six hours a day. Over 56 days, this uses 16,800 lux-hours of the 20,000 lux-hour allowance, leaving 3,200 lux-hours.

If a new crack appears, stop the display, photograph the crack and contact the lender before moving the sign.
